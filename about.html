Our Story

AA Kanuka Financial Services is a proudly South African financial services company established in June 2020 by two cousins, Ayanda and Aphumelela Kanuka.

The company is based in the small town of Komani (Queenstown), Eastern Cape — a tightly knit community that thrives on camaraderie, trust, and shared progress. It is within this environment of unity and collaboration that the business was born and continues to grow.

From inception, the company has been committed to delivering accessible, reliable, and professional financial services tailored to the needs of small to medium-sized enterprises.

Our Vision

Bridging the gap

The company was established after the two Directors identified a gap in the market: small to medium-sized businesses were often overlooked, faced unaffordable pricing structures, and received little to no guidance on financial compliance requirements.

Our vision was therefore born — to accommodate small to medium businesses through affordable financial services while educating our clients on the various compliance requirements companies must meet, why they are necessary, and the institutions that regulate them. We believe knowledge builds stronger and more sustainable businesses.

Our Mission

Empowering through knowledge

Our mission is to support and empower small businesses by equipping our staff with the latest updates in the financial services industry and staying informed on current affairs.

This ensures that we anticipate regulatory changes, mitigate risks, and remain proactive in serving the industries in which our clients operate.
