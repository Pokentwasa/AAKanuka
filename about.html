About Us

Built on trust. Driven by precision.

Established in June 2020 by Ayanda and Aphumelela Kanuka in Komani (Queenstown), Eastern Cape.

Our Story

What began as a local practice serving neighbours and small traders has grown into a trusted financial services partner for businesses across the province. We exist to bring institutional-grade financial discipline to entrepreneurs who are building something real — without the jargon, without the condescension, and without the corporate distance.

Every client engagement begins with understanding — your business model, your growth stage, your pain points. We then build a financial framework around your reality, not a generic template.
